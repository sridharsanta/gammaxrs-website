# Gamma XRS — pointers and stage specification

**The brand guide is `brand/index.html` in this repository — GX-BRAND-001, Revision A.** It is the
single source of truth for the marks, colour, typography, motion, art direction and voice. Do not
rebuild any of that from memory: several earlier explorations used a different mark, a different
palette and a different name, and they are superseded.

## The short version, so nothing gets rebuilt wrong

- **Name:** Gamma XRS. Title case in body copy; all caps belongs to the logotype only. Never "XRS"
  on its own. Products read "Gamma XRS Volume One".
- **Promise:** *Final pixel, on the day.* Verbatim, never paraphrased.
- **Symbol:** the Aperture — two opposing arrays of 17 spectral blades separated by a fixed 32u
  gate, sitting on a parabolic arch. Generated from the parameters in section 03, not drawn. A
  redraw that does not match those values is wrong.
- **Logotype:** Michroma, redrawn, vector artwork only. Never reset in another face.
- **Type:** Archivo (variable — the width axis carries hierarchy, 112–118 display, 100 body) and
  JetBrains Mono for every measured value.
- **Core colour:** Gamma Violet `#9721B0`; on dark grounds the logotype uses Gamma Violet Lift
  `#B84BD6`. Ground is Void `#080808`.
- **Two ramps:** Spectrum Prime on dark grounds, Spectrum Deep on light. Switch by ground, never
  by preference. Never place the gradient mark on a mid-tone.
- **Voice:** say the number. Banned: seamless, revolutionary, unlock, empower, game-changing,
  immersive, next-generation, leverage, solutions, magic.
- **Art direction:** never the wide empty volume with one figure for scale — the guide rules that
  out explicitly as the stock photograph of this industry.

## Atelier Stage

Footprint 12 ft × 10 ft. LED panel size, pixel pitch and rates are **not yet known** and ship as
bracketed placeholders.

## Open conflict to resolve

The original brief named the stages **gammaxrs** (Signature) and **gammaxra** (Atelier), implying
a tier system with sub-lockups. GX-BRAND-001 has no tier system — it specifies five lockups and a
product-naming rule ("Gamma XRS Volume One"). The website follows the brand guide and names them
"Signature Stage" and "Atelier Stage" as products of Gamma XRS. If the tier lettering is wanted,
it is a change to the marks and needs executive sign-off under the guide's own governance rule A3.
